## 2. Placeholders to Update from the Admin Dashboard

Access the Admin Portal at [http://localhost:5173/admin](http://localhost:5173/admin) to replace any placeholder as new materials are ready:

### A. Replacing Sample Demonstration Projects (`/admin/projects`)
Currently, 6 demonstration concept templates are preloaded:
- `Website UI/UX Concept — Sample`
- `Mobile App Design Concept — Sample`
- `Brand Identity Concept — Sample`
- `Social Media Campaign Concept — Sample`
- `SEO Case Study Template — Sample`
- `E-Commerce Dashboard Concept — Sample`

**How to replace with real work:**
1. Log in to `/admin/projects`.
2. Edit or delete a sample concept.
3. Add your real project title, cover screenshot, client objectives, design process, and live Figma/Behance link.
4. Uncheck **"Mark as Sample Concept Placeholder"** to display it as verified client work.

---

### B. Uploading Official CV (`/admin/settings`)
1. Export your design resume to PDF.
2. In `/admin/settings`, locate the **Official CV Document (PDF)** section.
3. Click **"Upload PDF"** to upload the resume file.
4. Toggle **"Enable CV Download for visitors"** on.
5. Click **"Save Settings"**. The homepage "Download CV" button will immediately become active and downloadable.

---

### C. Adding Client Testimonials (`/admin/testimonials`)
1. In accordance with strict ethical standards, no fake testimonials have been invented.
2. When genuine client feedback or reviews are received, navigate to `/admin/testimonials`.
3. Click **"Add Testimonial"**, enter client name, role, company, and quote.
4. Check **"Verified Genuine Client"** and **"Publish on Website"** to make it publicly visible.

---

### D. Adding Social Profiles (`/admin/settings`)
When you establish your official profiles, enter your URLs in `/admin/settings`:
- Behance URL (`https://behance.net/username`)
- Dribbble URL (`https://dribbble.com/username`)
- LinkedIn URL (`https://linkedin.com/in/username`)
- Instagram URL (`https://instagram.com/username`)
